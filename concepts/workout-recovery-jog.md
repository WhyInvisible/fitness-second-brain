# Recovery jog (active recovery, not training)

## Purpose
Active recovery -- blood flow, lymphatic drainage, mental break. Not a "real" run.

## Structure
Short, very slow. 20-40 min.

## Effort
RPE 2/10. Slower than easy. Comfortable to hold conversation easily. Pfitz: <76% HRmax.

## When to use
Day after a hard session if you're sore. Optional.

## Common mistake
Making it not slow enough. If HR is above Z1, it's no longer recovery -- it's just a slow easy run, which has different intent.
