# Runner strength loading targets: RDL 1.5-1.8xBW, hip thrust 2xBW, squat 1.3-1.5xBW

Per Dicharry Ch 9, the runner-strength benchmarks worth working toward:

| Lift | Rep target | BW multiple | e.g. a 70 kg runner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Romanian deadlift | 8 reps | 1.5-1.8x BW | **105-125 kg** |
| Back / front squat | 6 reps | 1.3-1.5x BW | **90-105 kg** |
| Hip thrust | 6 reps | 2x BW | **140 kg** |

Work out the athlete's own numbers from the body weight in [profile.md](../profile.md).

## How to use these
- Long-term targets, not entry points -- multi-season project
- Calibrate "are we strong enough yet"
- 1x BW RDL -> obvious headroom
- 1.5x BW RDL -> diminishing returns, shift energy back into running

## Why force production beats size
Heavy + low rep + compound. **Bar speed never drops > 50% within a set. Never to failure. Never grinding.** Tempo smooth ~2.5 s each direction. Don't hold breath -- 360deg brace + exhale on drive. See [bar-speed-rule](bar-speed-rule.md).

A small force gain x 20,000 marathon steps = free stride length without added weight to carry.
