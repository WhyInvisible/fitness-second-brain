# Week of slow — reset protocol

A one-week reset to break [intensity blindness](intensity-blindness.md) and reset your perception of what "easy" actually feels like. Fitzgerald's analogue to a juice fast: not training itself, but a habit-breaker that prepares you to train better.

## Protocol

- **Every run for one week** at "embarrassingly slow" pace.
- **No watch, or watch on time-only.** No pace alerts, no HR alerts.
- Find a pace that feels **completely effortless, free from any strain**. Each time you catch yourself speeding up, slow back down.
- Sample schedules (Fitzgerald Table 1.1):
  - **Level 1:** 2 / 5 / 3 / 6 / 4 / 7 / 5 mi (across 7 days)
  - **Level 2:** 4 / 6 / 7 / —, build to 8 mi at end
  - **Level 3:** 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 mi
- Find a route or time of day where you won't be seen if you're embarrassed by the pace.

## What you'll notice

- **Day 1–2:** mental struggle. Thoughts wander, pace creeps back up, you have to consciously slow down. Pace will feel weirdly *uncomfortable* despite being physiologically easy — because the *psychology* of running fast is what feels normal.
- **Day 4–5:** the effortless pace starts to feel natural.
- **Day 7:** you've forgotten what the habitual moderate pace felt like, and the easy pace genuinely is easy.

After the week: you can re-introduce a structured plan with quality sessions, but now your "easy" pace is actually easy, and you can stop the [black-hole](black-hole-of-training-intensity.md) drift.

## When to use

- Before starting any new 80/20-style plan
- Whenever a fitness assessment reveals you're stalling (Juan Carlos case: 10K stuck at 52:30 → week of slow + 11 wk of 80/20 → 48:47)
- After a heavy block where moderate-intensity creep has accumulated
- After a race / break where you've lost calibration

## When NOT to use

- The week before a race (use a normal taper instead)
- During a peak block (you need the structured stimulus)

## Application

**Recommended placement:** the week between the foundation phase and the aerobic build in the [season plan](../plans/season-plan.md) — right before LT1 sessions start ramping. Use it to confirm the athlete's true easy pace (often noticeably slower than their habitual "easy" pace) and to set the HR ceiling for [hr-governs-easy-pace-governs-hard](hr-governs-easy-pace-governs-hard.md).
