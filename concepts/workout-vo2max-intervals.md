# VO2max intervals (Daniels I)

## Purpose
Raise the ceiling -- more oxygen -> faster top-end speed -> easier MP feels easier. Secondary priority for marathoners (Pfitz: "VO2max sessions are definitely a secondary consideration for a marathoner").

## Structure (Pfitzinger marathon-specific)
- Intervals 2-6 min in length = 800 m to 1,600 m
- Total per-session interval volume 5,000-10,000 m (typically 6,000-8,000 m)
- Common templates: 5 x 1,000 m / 4 x 1,200 m / 6 x 1,000 m / 4 x 1,600 m
- Rest = 50-90% of interval time

## Caps (Daniels rule)
- Single rep <= 5 min (longer reps slow without adding adaptation -- physiological VO2max ceiling is ~11 min total)
- Total I-pace in a session <= 8% of weekly mileage or 10K, whichever is less

## Pace = 5K race pace, NOT 3K pace
Per Pfitz: "running intervals much faster than 3,000- to 5,000-meter race pace also produces a smaller stimulus to improve your VO2max."

## Effort
[vVO2max pace](../MOCs/MOC-paces-zones.md), RPE 8-9/10 -- capped at the conservative 93-95% HRmax / 91-94% HRR. If "desperately hard," you're too fast.

## When to use
Marathon build phase, 1x per week max (alternating with threshold). Last VO2max session 10 days pre-race (per Pfitz's taper).

## Common mistake
Starting too fast. First interval should feel almost easy. If interval 3 is your fastest, you've paced it right.
