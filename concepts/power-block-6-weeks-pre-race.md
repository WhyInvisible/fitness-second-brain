# Dicharry's power block (6 weeks pre-race, RFD training)

Per Dicharry: "In the 6 weeks prior to key races, incorporate a weekly power workout." Rate-of-force-development (RFD) training builds speed *without* requiring much recovery -- fits cleanly into taper.

## Why it works
- Plyometric stimulus = neural adaptation (more recruitment, faster firing)
- Low metabolic cost = doesn't compete with running for recovery
- Quick gains visible in 4-6 weeks = perfectly timed for pre-race

## Placing the blocks
Count back 6 weeks from each key race in the [season plan](../plans/season-plan.md). A marathon build with a tune-up half usually gets two:
- **Block 1:** the 6 weeks before the tune-up race
- **Block 2:** the 6 weeks before the A-race (running into the taper)

Both: 1x weekly Power workout, alongside reduced strength volume.

## The exercises
Medball acceleration sprints, medball push-press, medball triple bound, box squat jumps, ninja squat jumps, split box jumps, dumbbell push-press, flip-flop sprints, incline sprints, stair bounds. Each gets its own exercise note in exercises/.
