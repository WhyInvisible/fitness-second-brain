# 3-week taper: cut volume, hold intensity

Pfitzinger's research-backed template: -20-30% volume (Wk-3), -40% (Wk-2), -60% (race week). Intensity sessions get *shorter*, not slower -- strides, short tempos, one final VO2max session 10 days out.

## Expected gain
Studies show 2-3% race-day performance improvement from a proper taper. For a 3:00 marathoner that's 3.5-5 minutes -- comparable to weeks of training.

## What goes wrong
- Cutting intensity too much -> loss of sharpness, race feels sluggish
- Cutting volume too little -> fatigue carries into race
- Adding "just one more" hard session -> recovery is incomplete on race day
