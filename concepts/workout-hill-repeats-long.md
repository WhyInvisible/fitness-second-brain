# Hill repeats (long, Norwegian Zone 4 style)

## Purpose
Leg strength, power, running economy, low impact relative to flat speed work. In the Norwegian system, this is the weekly Zone 4 -- the one high-intensity aerobic stimulus per week.

## Structure (short hard, classic)
6-10 x 60-90 second uphill at hard effort, jog down recovery. Pick a 4-8% grade.

## Structure (Norwegian volume)
20 x 200 m hill repeats with 70-second jog-back recovery. Lactate ~8 mmol/L. Used by elite Norwegians (e.g. Kalle Berglund Saturday session) as the weekly Zone 4 dose.

## Effort
RPE 8-9/10 on the way up. Recovery is fully easy on the way down.

## When to use
Base build / aerobic build / marathon build phases. 1x per week. Excellent for runners avoiding flat speed work (less impact, builds posterior chain).

## Common mistake
Sprinting reps 1-3 then dying. Pace by RPE, leave 1 rep in the tank on each.
