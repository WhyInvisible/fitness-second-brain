# Race performance sets training pace, not the other way round

"Prove to me in a race that you are ready to train faster." Bump VDOT only after a race performance justifies it -- or after 4-6 weeks at one level with no perceived stress.

Per Daniels 2022.

## Why
The athlete's perception of fitness is unreliable. Wanting to run faster ≠ being able to recover from running faster. A race is the test that strips the wishful thinking.

## Practical rule
- After a benchmark race (5K, 10K, half), recalculate VDOT and pace zones (see [paces-zones.md](../MOCs/MOC-paces-zones.md))
- Without a race: hold current VDOT for 4-6 weeks at minimum before considering a bump
- Never chase paces you haven't earned in competition or test

## The athlete's anchor
Keep three things on record: the current VDOT and the race it came from (in [profile.md](../profile.md)), and the target VDOT the A-race goal implies (in [goals.md](../goals.md)). The next benchmark race or time trial in the [season plan](../plans/season-plan.md) is the next planned recalibration.
