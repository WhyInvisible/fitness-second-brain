# Sub-threshold (LT1 / Norwegian sub-threshold)

## Purpose
The workhorse aerobic-quality stimulus. Sits between MP and threshold -- high enough to drive lactate clearance + threshold adaptation, low enough to do 2x per week sustainably. Per Kelemen et al. 2024, this is the bedrock of the Norwegian system.

## Structure (verbatim from elite use)
- 5 x 6 min @ 1 min jog rest
- OR 5 x 2 km @ 1 min jog rest
- OR 6-8 x 1000 m @ 1 min jog rest

Lactate kept at ~2.0-2.5 mmol/L.

## Effort
[LT1 pace](../MOCs/MOC-paces-zones.md), RPE 5-6/10. "Firm and controlled" -- can still speak in short full sentences. Faster than easy, slower than threshold.

## When to use
Aerobic build phase onwards, **2x per week** (this is the big shift from older training models). Often Tue + Thu.

## Common mistake
Running it as threshold. If you can only speak single words, too fast. If you finish gassed, too fast. The whole point is to repeat next session in 48 hours.
