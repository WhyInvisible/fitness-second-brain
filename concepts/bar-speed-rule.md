# Bar speed rule: never drops > 50% in a set

> "If your form doesn't break down and your lift speed doesn't decrease more than 50 percent within the set, add weight." -- Dicharry, *Running Rewired*

## What this means
- Lifting/lowering tempo: smooth, ~2.5 s each direction
- The set is over when bar speed drops > 50%
- Never grind, never to failure
- Holds: brace 360deg around the spine, exhale on the drive. **No held breath.**

## Why for runners
Grinding reps to failure produce hypertrophy + maximum fatigue -- neither is desired. Stopping at the speed cliff produces force adaptation with minimal hypertrophy and recoverable fatigue.

## Applies to
Every loaded lift: RDL, squat, hip thrust, OHP, bench press. The rule is the autoregulation.

## How to gauge bar speed without a velocity tracker
By feel + by sound. Rep 1 has a certain rhythm. When rep 4 takes noticeably longer to complete the concentric, that's the 50% drop signal. End the set.
