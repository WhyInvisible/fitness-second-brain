# Consistency beats intensity

A boring, repeatable week done 12 weeks in a row beats a perfect week done 3 times.

The single highest-leverage training principle. Adaptations accumulate from consistent stimulus over months, not from heroic individual sessions.

## Practical application
- Choose the week structure you can actually do every week, not the one that looks best on paper.
- Plan around your actual life (work, sleep, social) rather than around an idealised schedule.
- Missing a planned session is a smaller cost than burning out by overcommitting.

## When this principle wins
- Choosing 5 runs/week sustainable over 7 runs/week heroic
- Picking a mileage ceiling you can hold through travel and bad weeks
- Saying no to a "perfect" training block that doesn't fit your constraints
