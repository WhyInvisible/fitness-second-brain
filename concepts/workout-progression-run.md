# Progression run

## Purpose
Discipline of starting slow and finishing strong, late-race fatigue resistance.

## Structure
Start at easy pace, gradually accelerate. Final 1/3 at MP or slightly faster.

Example 12 km:
- 4 km easy
- 4 km steady
- 4 km at MP

## Effort
Climbs through RPE 3 -> 6 over the run.

## When to use
Marathon build phase, 1x per month max as a long-run variation.

## Common mistake
Going too fast in the first third -- then can't finish the progression.
