# Cutbacks every 3-4 weeks

A 20-30% volume reduction every 4th week prevents injuries and lets adaptation consolidate.

## Why
Continuous progressive overload is unsustainable -- fatigue accumulates faster than fitness. A planned cutback week is when the body actually banks the adaptations from the preceding 3 weeks of stimulus.

## How
- Reduce weekly mileage by 20-30% (not intensity)
- Keep the quality session, just shorter
- Skip the longest long run; cap at ~70% of typical
- One easy day extra than normal
