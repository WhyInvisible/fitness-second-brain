# Threshold (LT2 / Pfitz LT tempo / Daniels T-pace)

## Purpose
Raise actual lactate threshold (the rate-limiter for half-marathon and below). Higher per-session adaptation than LT1, but higher recovery cost -- used less frequently.

## Structure (Norwegian interval form)
- 10-12 x 1000 m @ 1 min jog rest
- OR 25 x 400 m @ 30 s rest
- OR 45/15s on/off intervals

Lactate ~3.0-3.5 mmol/L.

## Structure (Pfitzinger continuous tempo -- preferred for marathoners)
- 20-40 min continuous at LT pace
- Sandwiched by 2-3 km warm-up + 1-2 km cool-down
- Total session 12-18 km

Per Pfitzinger: "the marathon is one long continuous run, and tempo runs simulate marathon conditions more closely." For marathon training, alternate between Norwegian-interval form (one week) and Pfitz continuous tempo (the next).

## Structure (Daniels cruise-interval variant)
1- or 2-mile reps at T pace with 1-2 min rests (5:1 work:rest ratio).

## Cap (Daniels rule)
Total T-pace running in one session <= 10% of weekly mileage. At 80 km/wk -> max 8 km at T pace. See [per-zone-weekly-volume-caps](per-zone-weekly-volume-caps.md).

## Effort
[LT2 pace](../MOCs/MOC-paces-zones.md), RPE 7/10. Half-marathon effort. Could hold for ~60 min in a race. Speech in short phrases only.

## When to use
Aerobic build phase onwards, ~1x per week, typically alternating weekly placement with VO2max work in marathon-build.

## Common mistake
Treating LT2 as VO2max. If you're gasping by interval 4, you're 5-10 sec/km too fast.
