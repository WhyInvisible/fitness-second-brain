# Track what matters; ignore the rest

Distance, time, HR, RPE, sleep, soreness. Not every metric your watch shows.

## What's worth tracking
- Run: distance, time, average HR, RPE
- Strength: sets / reps / weights
- Recovery: sleep duration, morning HR, perceived freshness
- Trend over time: weekly mileage, body weight, race times

## What to ignore
- Most "training effect" / "training load" / "performance condition" scores from watches -- algorithmically generated, don't reflect your reality.
- Day-to-day fluctuations in HRV without trend context.
- Strava kudos and segment leaderboards (motivation tools, not training data).
