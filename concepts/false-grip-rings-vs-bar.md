# False grip: the foundational muscle-up skill

The false grip is the hand position that makes a strict ring muscle-up possible at Level 5 instead of Level 6. **Wrist on top of the ring**, not palm.

## How to set it

1. Place wrist crook (pinky side) on top of the ring or bar.
2. Wrap hand around so palm sits **directly under** the ring (NOT to the side, NOT too far up).
3. Grip as tight as possible.

## Why it matters

In the muscle-up transition phase (between top of pull-up and bottom of dip), your hand has to move from in-front-of-chest to under-the-armpit position. With a normal grip, the lever arm at the wrist is long and the transition is heavy. **False grip puts the wrist over the ring, shortening the lever arm dramatically.** The transition becomes possible at much lower pulling/dipping strength.

## The threshold

**With correct false grip: 5 chest-to-rings pull-ups + 5 ring dips with full ROM = you can do a strict ring muscle-up.**

Without false grip (Level 6 "no-FG MU"): you need significantly more shoulder + elbow strength to brute-force the transition.

## False grip on bar vs rings

- **Rings:** more natural — the ring rolls around the wrist crook, allowing the transition. Recommended starting point.
- **Bar:** harder — the bar can't rotate, so you have to slide the hand into position. Strict bar MU (Level 7) is harder than strict ring MU (Level 5) for this reason.

## Common errors

1. **Poor wrist flexibility** — wrist won't bend enough to get false grip. Fix: wrist mobility (wrist circles, Asian squat, rice bucket) 5 min daily for weeks.
2. **Poor grip strength / can't hang in false grip** — grip fails before training stimulus is achieved. Fix: false-grip dead hangs (3 × 10 → 20 s) in warm-up + rice bucket + wrist roller work.
3. **Hand position wrong** — palm too far up the ring or too far down. Should be palm **directly under** the ring.
4. **Wrists tearing up** — common when starting. Fix: reduce frequency to allow callus formation; use wristbands; never use gloves (kills grip strength).

## Active when?

**Outside a marathon block.** The muscle-up ladder belongs in an off-season skill phase, and only once the pull-up and dip prerequisites are comfortably met (a conservative bar: 3 × 12 strict pull-ups and 3 × 12 dips). Until then, false grip work is not a priority.

When introduced, the progression sequence is:
1. **False-grip dead hang** — 3 × 10 → 20 s
2. **False-grip pull-up** — 3 × 5 (much harder than regular pull-up)
3. **Muscle-up negative** — start at top of support, lower slowly through transition into hang. 3 × 3 × 5–8 s eccentric
4. **Banded / assisted muscle-up** — band under feet or partner spotting
5. **Strict ring muscle-up with false grip** (Level 5) — first clean rep
6. Later: no-false-grip muscle-up (Level 6), strict bar MU (Level 7)
