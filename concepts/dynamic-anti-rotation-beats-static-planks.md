# Dynamic anti-rotation core beats static planks for runners

Per Dicharry: "our end goal is not to impress our friends with a 4-minute plank, but to improve mobility while running."

## What runners actually need
Running is a rotational sport -- counter-rotation between the shoulders and hips with every stride. Core training should target this pattern.

## Hierarchy
1. **Base layer (daily):** [mcgill-big-3](mcgill-big-3.md) -- back-safe stability
2. **Once comfortable:** layer in dynamic anti-rotation
   - Thread-the-needle plank
   - Super swiss side plank
   - Bear walk
   - Donkey toes
3. **Bolt-on (anytime):** loaded carries (see [carries-train-anti-lateral-flexion](carries-train-anti-lateral-flexion.md))

## What to skip
- Chasing long plank times (4-min planks don't transfer to running)
- Spinal flexion work (crunches, sit-ups) -- not back-safe if the athlete has a lower-back history (see [profile.md](../profile.md) → Injuries and niggles)
