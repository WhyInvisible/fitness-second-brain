# Daniels' per-zone weekly volume caps (T 10%, I 8%, R 5%)

Per Daniels 2022:
- **T-pace (threshold):** <= 10% of weekly mileage in one session
- **I-pace (VO2max intervals):** <= 8% of weekly mileage, or 10K, whichever is less
- **R-pace (repetition):** <= 5% of weekly mileage, or 5 mi (8 km), whichever is less

## Why these caps exist
Each zone produces diminishing returns past a threshold of weekly volume. Past the cap, additional volume produces fatigue without proportional adaptation.

## Worked example (hypothetical: 80 km/wk during a marathon build)
- Max T-pace per session: 8 km
- Max I-pace per session: 6.4 km (~5 x 1200 m or 4 x 1600 m)
- Max R-pace per session: 4 km (well within the 8 km hard cap)

## How to apply
- Plan each quality session against these caps
- If a session would exceed the cap, split into two sessions OR shorten to cap
- The caps are absolute, not aspirational -- going over consistently invites overtraining
