# Vary the stress

Easy distance, threshold, VO2max, long run. Each builds different things. A week with all-easy or all-hard is a wasted week.

**Polarisation caveat (Fitzgerald):** "variety" doesn't mean "everything is moderately stressful." It means very easy *plus* truly hard, with the [black hole](black-hole-of-training-intensity.md) middle deliberately avoided. The Salzburg study confirmed this: the "threshold" group (46/54/0 — lots of moderate) was the *worst* performer. Polarised (68/6/26 — easy + hard, no middle) was the best.

## The four pillars
- **Easy volume** -> aerobic base, capillarisation, mitochondrial density
- **Threshold / LT** -> lactate clearance, sustainable pace
- **VO2max / intervals** -> ceiling, oxygen uptake
- **Long run** -> endurance, glycogen, fat oxidation, mental durability

## Failure modes
- All-easy weeks: aerobic only, no threshold or speed -> plateau
- All-hard weeks: insufficient recovery -> overtraining
- Same stress every week: adaptation curve flattens

## Practical application
A balanced marathon-build week includes: 1 long run, 1-2 sub-threshold sessions, 1 threshold OR VO2max, 1 short power stimulus (hills/strides), rest easy mileage.
