# Easy run (Z2 / conversational)

## Purpose
Aerobic base, capillary density, mitochondrial development. The bedrock of marathon training. ~80% of weekly volume should be at this effort.

## Structure
Continuous, steady, comfortable. Could hold a conversation in full sentences.

## Effort
[Z2 HR / easy pace](../MOCs/MOC-paces-zones.md), RPE 3-4/10.

## Duration
30-75 min typical.

## Common mistake
Running too fast. If HR drifts above Z2 cap or you can only speak in short phrases -- slow down. Easy runs that creep into Z3 sabotage the next quality session and add fatigue without aerobic benefit.

## When to use
Every week. The majority of weekly volume. See [80-20-rule](80-20-rule.md).
