# Marathon goal wins conflicts

This is the marathon-specific case of [a-goal-wins-conflicts](a-goal-wins-conflicts.md): when the A-race in [goals.md](../goals.md) is a marathon and two disciplines collide, marathon prep gets the call until race day. After the race, the athlete re-ranks and the other disciplines get their turn.

## Why this matters
A hybrid athlete might train running, strength, calisthenics and yoga or mobility in the same week. They will conflict: scheduling, recovery budget, CNS demand. Without a clear hierarchy, the athlete dithers and every discipline ends up half-done.

## A typical order during a marathon build
1. Marathon training (running + supporting strength)
2. Recovery + sleep
3. Yoga / mobility (supports recovery)
4. Calisthenics skill work (maintenance only)
5. Any other bolt-on (e.g. climbing grip work), lowest priority

Adjust the lower rungs to the athlete's own B- and C-goals in [goals.md](../goals.md); the top two don't move during the build.

## Example conflicts
- Heavy leg day clashes with the day before the long run -> skip leg day, do mobility
- Skill calisthenics session tempting on long-run day -> skip, long run wins
- Grip or upper-body session feels good but a joint is twingey -> skip, recovery wins

When the plan makes one of these calls, say so in plain words, as [a-goal-wins-conflicts](a-goal-wins-conflicts.md) asks.
