# Loaded carries train anti-lateral-flexion (runner's underused weapon)

Per Dicharry, loaded carries (overhead, waiter, suitcase, farmer) build postural strength + anti-lateral-flexion core + grip in one. The carry is an underused weapon for runners.

## The four carries
- **Overhead carry** -- weight overhead, walk. Trains scap stability + thoracic extension.
- **Waiter carry** -- weight at shoulder height, walk. Anti-rotation, scap stability.
- **Suitcase carry** -- weight in one hand, walk. **Anti-lateral-flexion** -- directly trains the late-marathon trunk-wobble pattern.
- **Farmer carry** -- weight in both hands, walk. Grip + postural endurance.

## Why for marathoners
At km 30+, fatigue shows up as trunk wobble -- torso starts swinging side-to-side because the lateral core gives up. Suitcase carries directly train resistance to this pattern.

## How to dose
- 3-4 sets of 30 m walks
- Heaviest weight you can hold WITHOUT shoulder shrug or torso lean
- Bolt onto any leg or pull day
