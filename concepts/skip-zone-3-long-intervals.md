# Skip Zone 3 long intervals during base

Long intervals at 4-8 mmol lactate (Zone 3 -- "intense aerobic intervals" like 3 x 8 min at 5K pace) generate too much lactate for the recovery cost. The Norwegian system explicitly avoids this during base period.

## What to do instead
- **LT2 (threshold)** -- lower lactate (~3.5 mmol), shorter intervals (400-1000 m), similar pace. More volume, less cost.
- **Zone 4 (anaerobic)** -- very short (200 m or 10-12 sec), race pace or faster. Builds anaerobic capacity + power without metabolic carnage.

## Why
Long Zone 3 intervals fall into the worst-case combination: long enough to accumulate lactate, hard enough to compromise the next session, not hard enough to drive specific anaerobic adaptation. They're the medium-hard "grey zone" amplified.

## When Zone 3 is appropriate
- Race-specific work for 5K/10K race prep
- Short interval (2-5 min) work in marathon-build (managed via Daniels' weekly caps)
- Not for marathon base period
