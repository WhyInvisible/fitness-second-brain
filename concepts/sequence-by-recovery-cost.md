# Sequence by recovery cost

Hard run -> easy day -> strength -> easy/yoga -> long run -> restorative. The rhythm is more important than any single session.

## Why
Each session should be done when the body is recovered enough to extract its full adaptation. A hard run done on tired legs = less adaptation, more injury risk.

## The rhythm in practice (example week)
- Mon: easy + leg day setup
- Tue: easy AM + leg day PM (hard CNS)
- Wed: easy / light (recovery from Tue)
- Thu: easy AM strides + upper PM
- Fri: upper AM (running rest day)
- Sat: long run (well-rested from Friday rest)

Shift the days to fit the athlete's week in [current-week.md](../plans/current-week.md); keep the order.
- Sun: restorative

## When the rhythm breaks
Travel, illness, life. Don't try to maintain perfect rhythm -- adjust the next 2-3 days to restore the easy-hard alternation.
