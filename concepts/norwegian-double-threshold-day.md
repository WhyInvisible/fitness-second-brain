# Norwegian double-threshold day (AM LT1 + PM LT2)

The signature structure of elite Norwegian distance training: morning session at LT1 (longer intervals, lower lactate) + afternoon session 5-6 h later at LT2 (shorter intervals, slightly higher lactate). Total ~2 x 10-12 km of Zone 2 work in one day.

## The principle
Splitting threshold work across morning + afternoon allows much more weekly threshold volume than doing one big session, because each individual session stays sub-maximal.

## The session pair (Kalle Berglund example, 2018-19)
- AM: 5 x 6 min @ 2.5 mmol/L, 1 min jog rest
- PM (5-6 h later): 10 x 1000 m @ 3.5 mmol/L, 1 min jog rest

## For amateurs
Don't try the full double -- the recovery budget is too tight for someone with a job and other disciplines to fit around running. The amateur adaptation ("Norwegian Singles") is: 2x weekly LT1 + 1x weekly LT2, all single-session, all on different days.

When the [season plan](../plans/season-plan.md) includes a threshold-focused phase, build it on the singles version.
