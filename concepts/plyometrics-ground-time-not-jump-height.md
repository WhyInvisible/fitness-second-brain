# Plyometrics: ground time, not jump height

Per Dicharry Ch 9: box height <= knee / hip. Land full foot. Double-bouncing = spring is gone = rest. Tall box jumps actively work *against* runners by increasing ground-contact time. Run-specific plyometrics belong in the 6 weeks pre-race.

## Why short ground time matters
Running economy = energy returned per stride. Short ground contact = elastic energy returned. Tall jumps train CONCENTRIC force (push), which is the opposite of what runners need (quick eccentric-concentric coupling).

## What looks right
- Mid-shin to knee-height boxes only
- Land flat foot, immediately rebound
- Stop the set when bounce quality degrades (double-bouncing = elastic recoil gone)
- 4-6 sets of 4-6 reps, full recovery between sets

## When to do it
Power block: 6 weeks pre-race. See [power-block-6-weeks-pre-race](power-block-6-weeks-pre-race.md).
