# Mobility is daily, flexibility is bonus

A 5-minute hip / ankle / thoracic routine done every day will help your running more than one 90-min class per week.

## Why
- Mobility is neurological -- needs frequent reinforcement to stick
- Flexibility (tissue length change) takes 3-min holds, 4-5x/wk, for 10 weeks (per Dicharry)
- Once-a-week long sessions don't accumulate enough stimulus for either

## What 5 minutes looks like
- Ankle circles + calf bounces
- Hip CARs (controlled articular rotations)
- T-spine rotation
- One 30-sec dynamic stretch for any specific tight area
