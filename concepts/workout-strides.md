# Strides

## Purpose
Neuromuscular activation, leg turnover, running economy. Low fatigue cost.

## Structure (Daniels protocol)
6-8 x 15-20 sec at R pace (~mile race pace), 45-60 sec full recovery between. At end (or middle) of an easy run. Full protocol + cues in strides exercise file.

## Effort
~85% effort, smooth and tall. Not sprints -- comfortable speed with good form. Practise the 180 steps/min cadence here.

## When to use
**2 days per week minimum, every week from base phase through taper.** Drop only in race week. Per Daniels, strides are non-negotiable maintenance for economy.

## Common mistake
Sprinting. Strides should feel relaxed and fast, not all-out.
