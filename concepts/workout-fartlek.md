# Fartlek (unstructured speed play)

## Purpose
Flexible quality work, fun variety, used when terrain or schedule prevents structured intervals.

## Structure
Unstructured fast/slow alternations during a run. E.g.:
- "1 min hard, 2 min easy x 8"
- Play with lampposts (sprint to next lamppost, jog two)
- Hard on uphills, easy on flats and downhills

## Effort
Mixed -- efforts can be threshold to VO2max.

## When to use
As a substitute for structured intervals, or when fresh and want play. Once a week max.

## Common mistake
Turning every fartlek into a max-effort session. Vary intensity -- that's the point.
