# The A-goal wins conflicts

Most athletes train for more than one thing at once: a race plus strength, a sport plus mobility, a skill plus general fitness. When two of them compete for the same recovery or the same day, the **A-goal in goals.md wins**, and the plan should say so explicitly instead of quietly compromising both.

## Practical application

- Name the A-goal (one only) in [goals.md](../goals.md). Everything else is a B- or C-goal, maintained rather than pushed during the A-goal's build.
- In the weeks that matter most for the A-goal (the peak and the taper), cut B-goal work to maintenance first.
- When the agent makes a call because of this rule, it says so in plain words, e.g. "dropping Thursday's heavy legs because the long run is the priority this week".
- After the A-goal event, re-rank. The off-season is when B-goals get their turn.
