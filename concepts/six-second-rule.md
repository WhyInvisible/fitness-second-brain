# Daniels' 6-second rule (between adjacent pace zones)

For faster runners, between adjacent zones, paces differ by ~6 sec/400 m. So R is 6 sec faster than I, I is 6 sec faster than T.

## Why it's useful
Quick mental check on whether your prescribed paces are internally consistent. If T-pace is 5:00/km, I-pace should be ~4:24/km (6 sec/400 = 15 sec/km faster), R-pace another 6 sec/400 faster = ~4:00/km.

## When to use it
- Re-deriving paces after a benchmark race without retyping VDOT tables
- Sanity-checking session prescriptions in workouts
- Quickly adjusting target paces when running on hills or in heat

## Caveats
The 6-second rule is for trained runners. Slower runners (VDOT < 35) have wider gaps between zones. Check the athlete's current VDOT in [profile.md](../profile.md): comfortably above 35, the 6-second rule holds.
