# Listen to morning HRV / RHR / how you feel on the warm-up

If two of those three are off, the session you planned is probably the wrong one today.

## The three signals
1. **Morning HRV** -- most sports watches / wearables track this; trend over 7 days matters more than any single reading
2. **Morning resting HR** -- 5+ bpm above baseline = signal
3. **First 5 min of warm-up** -- legs feel like lead? HR climbing on easy jog? Breathing harder than normal?

## Decision matrix
- 0 signals off: proceed as planned
- 1 signal off: proceed but dial back intensity 10-20%
- 2 signals off: convert to easy run or rest
- 3 signals off: rest day

## What this isn't
Permission to skip every hard session. The point is autoregulation, not avoidance. Many days you'll feel "off" but be fine to train normally.
