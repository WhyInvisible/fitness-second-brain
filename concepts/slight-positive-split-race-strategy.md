# Slight positive split, not even splits

For sub-elite marathoners, slow-twitch fibre fatigue forces fast-twitch recruitment in the second half -- economy drops ~1% per half. Target the first half ~0.5-1.5% faster than overall pace.

## Worked example (hypothetical 3:30 target)
- Target finish: 3:30:00 (even split would be 1:45:00 per half)
- First half: ~1:44:15 (4:56/km)
- Second half: ~1:45:45 (5:01/km)

Plug the athlete's own target from [goals.md](../goals.md) into the same ~0.5-1.5% first-half margin.

## Why even splits often fail
The "even splits" advice assumes constant economy, which doesn't hold in marathon distance for sub-elites. Aiming for even splits often means starting too slow -- leaving time on the road that can't be recovered in the second half.

## How to execute
- First 5 km: settle into target pace, don't push
- Km 5-21: lock in the slightly-fast first-half pace
- Km 21-35: hold pace as economy degrades
- Km 35-42: hold form, accept the natural fade
