# Cut strength volume in peak weeks

During highest run volume, drop strength to 1x maintenance session. In taper, very light. In race week, none.

## Why

Peak running volume already takes the full CNS / recovery budget. Heavy strength on top = compromised long runs = bad trade.

## Template for a marathon build

Map these onto the weeks in the [season plan](../plans/season-plan.md):

- Peak build weeks: 1x maintenance leg session/week
- Taper (final 2-3 weeks): very light
- Race week: none

## What "maintenance" means

- Same compound lifts, ~80% of recent best
- Reps 3-5
- Leave 3+ reps in the tank -- never to failure
- No PR attempts

## Matthews' related principle: heavy IS the maintenance signal

Matthews Ch 16 makes a parallel argument from the OTHER direction — natural lifters CUTTING (calorie deficit for fat loss) should KEEP lifting heavy. "The name of the game is muscle preservation," and that requires continuing to overload the muscles. Matthews notes that most lifters see an initial strength dip when they move from a bulk to a cut, but that heavy training lets them rebuild to roughly where they started with little or no muscle loss (paraphrased from Matthews, *Bigger Leaner Stronger*, Ch 16).

The takeaway for a marathon build: maintenance during peak weeks doesn't mean "do light circuit work." It means "fewer sets at the same heavy load, never to failure." The heavy stimulus is what keeps the muscle. Drop to circuit-style 15-rep work and you'll lose strength faster.

## Protein matters MORE in peak weeks, not less

Per [sufficient-protein-1-gram-per-pound](sufficient-protein-1-gram-per-pound.md). In a calorie deficit (which most marathon training effectively is, due to high energy expenditure) protein protects muscle. Bump to 1.8-2.0 g/kg during marathon peak weeks, not down. E.g. a hypothetical 75 kg runner → roughly 135-150 g/day in peak weeks; use the athlete's own weight from [profile.md](../profile.md).

## Decision tree for any given peak week

| Body signals | Strength session |
|---|---|
| Good (sleep solid, RHR normal, no fatigue) | Run the 1× maintenance leg session |
| Borderline (mildly tired, sleep OK, mileage near peak) | Cut to 2 lifts only (squat + RDL), 3 sets each, 80% load |
| Compromised (sleep poor, RHR elevated, run sessions feel hard) | Skip strength this week. Light yoga / mobility instead. |
| Race week | Skip ALL strength regardless |
