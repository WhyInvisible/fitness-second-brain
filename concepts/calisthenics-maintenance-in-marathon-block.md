# Calisthenics during marathon block — active integration, not pure maintenance

**Revised framing:** the original "maintenance only" stance was too conservative. Most calisthenics work has a safe-now version that fits inside the marathon block without competing for recovery — *if* you separate high-CNS from low-CNS work and bias toward the latter during marathon training.

## What CAN actively progress during marathon block (low CNS cost)

- **Pull-up volume** — work toward 15 strict (muscle-up gate). Low CNS, sets up a muscle-up attempt after the race.
- **Dip volume** — same logic, second muscle-up gate.
- **Handstand stages 0–4** (wall-supported variants, kick-ups to wall). Sommer recommends near-daily handstand practice as the exception to skill-when-fresh.
- **L-sit progression** through tuck → one-leg → straddle. Low CNS, high core return.
- **Hollow body + arch body holds** — Sommer's FSAS foundation. Build to 60s + 60s.
- **Wrist conditioning** (Sommer WRS/1–4) before any handstand work.
- **False grip familiarisation** — light volume only, builds toward muscle-up.

## What stays MAINTENANCE-ONLY during marathon block (high CNS cost or high tendon risk)

- **Front lever / back lever / planche straight-arm work** — high tendon cost ([connective-tissue-adaptation-3-12-months](connective-tissue-adaptation-3-12-months.md)). Stage 0–1 maintenance only.
- **One-arm chin-up progression** — same tendon-risk reasoning + elbow.
- **Press handstand** — defer (years out anyway).
- **Sommer's full 12-week mastery templates** — they assume calisthenics is primary; don't run them during marathon block.
- **Heavy ring work** (ring muscle-up attempts, ring lever progressions) — defer.

## What SCALES DOWN through the season

Map onto the phases of the [season plan](../plans/season-plan.md):

| Phase | Posture |
|---|---|
| Rebuild / base build | Active progression — skill ladder advances 1 step per 4–6 weeks |
| Any bodyweight-only stretch (travel, no gym) | Heightened — ideal time for skill consolidation |
| Aerobic build | Maintenance only — hold current stage, no new progression attempts |
| Marathon-specific build → taper | Minimum: daily Big 3 + glute reactivation + one short skill-touch/wk |
| Race week | Just the essentials |

## Why this is the better framing

- The original "pure maintenance" assumption treated calisthenics as a single CNS-cost bucket. Reality: handstand wall holds + L-sit + pull-up volume are LOW CNS (and Sommer treats handstand as near-daily). Front lever progression is HIGH CNS (skip it).
- A multi-discipline athlete usually wants balance. Pure maintenance feels like neglect; balanced integration uses the calisthenics time deliberately.
- Marathon goal still wins on conflicts ([marathon-goal-wins-conflicts](marathon-goal-wins-conflicts.md)) — but most calisthenics work doesn't actually conflict if you choose the right subset.
