# Restorative yoga after long runs

Yin / restorative the evening after a long run is one of the best recovery tools you have.

## Why
- Long passive holds (2-5 min) release fascia tension built up over 20+ km
- Parasympathetic activation accelerates recovery
- Hip flexor / hamstring / lower back targets are exactly what running tightens

## Practical
- 20-30 min, evening of the long run
- Hip flexor opens, supine hamstring, supine spinal twist, child's pose
- Light or no music; this is recovery, not exercise

## Why not a vinyasa instead
A vinyasa raises HR, demands muscular work, blunts recovery. Save the strong practices for non-long-run days.
