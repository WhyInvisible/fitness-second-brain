# Marathon pace doesn't need to be practised much

A few longer marathon-pace efforts in the peak phase are enough. Marathon fitness is overwhelmingly aerobic base + lactate threshold.

## Why
Marathon pace is a comfortable aerobic effort. The adaptations that produce a good marathon are:
- Aerobic base (the bedrock -- easy mileage)
- Lactate threshold (the ceiling -- LT1/LT2 work)
- Glycogen + fat oxidation (long runs)
- Pacing discipline (a few MP rehearsals)

Spending too much weekly volume at MP exact pace is wasted: it's hard enough to compromise recovery but not specific enough to produce marathon-unique adaptation.

## What's enough
Per Pfitzinger: **4 MP-segment long runs** across the marathon-build phase. See [mp-runs-inside-long-run](mp-runs-inside-long-run.md) for the schedule.
