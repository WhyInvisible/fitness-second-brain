# Assess individual alignment (Craig's test), don't cue universal

Per Dicharry Ch 8: some bodies have femoral or shin torsion that makes "feet straight, knees over second toe" anatomically impossible. Forcing them into universal alignment is what causes the injury.

## Craig's test
Lie prone, knee bent to 90deg. Rotate the leg internally/externally to find the position where the femoral head sits centred in the hip socket (palpate the greater trochanter). That position is YOUR neutral hip rotation -- which determines your natural foot angle in standing.

## What it reveals
- Some people: feet at 11/1 o'clock is correct for their bones
- Others: 10/2 or 9/3
- Forcing the "standard" 12/12 = chronic knee/hip pain

## Application
If the athlete hasn't been tested, **run it before heavy squat / single-leg work.** A slightly externally rotated stance (feet at 11/1) may be exactly what their bones want. Don't guess -- assess.
