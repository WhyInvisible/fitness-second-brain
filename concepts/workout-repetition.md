# Repetition (Daniels R)

## Purpose
Speed, anaerobic power, running economy at fast paces. Trains the neuromuscular system without the aerobic cost of intervals.

## Structure
- 200-600 m reps at current mile / 1500 race pace (about 6 sec/400 faster than I pace -- see [six-second-rule](six-second-rule.md))
- Recovery is 2-3x the work duration as an easy jog -- full recovery is the point

## Caps (Daniels rule)
- Single rep <= 2 min
- Total R-pace running in a session <= 5% of weekly mileage or 5 mi (8 km), whichever is less

## Effort
Fast but not max. Should feel mechanically smooth. Daniels: "if you're struggling, good mechanics go out the window -- and the purpose of R is good mechanics at speed."

## When to use
Phase 2 (Daniels' IQ phase) before VO2max work; maintained at lower volume through later phases. For marathoners, 1x per month is enough.

## Common mistake
Treating R as VO2max -- cutting recovery short. The recovery has to be long enough that the next rep is fast and clean.
