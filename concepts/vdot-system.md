# VDOT system: race-derived pace prescription

Daniels' VDOT system translates a single race performance into prescribed paces for all five training intensities (E/M/T/I/R). VDOT is roughly equivalent to VO2max but expressed as a single integer.

## How it works
- Run any benchmark race (5K, 10K, half, marathon)
- Look up your VDOT in Daniels' tables
- The table also gives target paces for each training zone

## The athlete's anchor
- Current VDOT: from the most recent benchmark race or time trial recorded in [profile.md](../profile.md)
- Target VDOT: the value that matches the A-race target in [goals.md](../goals.md)
- All pace prescriptions in [paces-zones.md](../MOCs/MOC-paces-zones.md) derived from these

## When to update
After every benchmark race. Schedule a tune-up race or time trial mid-build (see the [season plan](../plans/season-plan.md)) as a planned recalibration.

## The shortcut: 6-second rule
Between adjacent zones (R -> I -> T), paces differ by ~6 sec/400 m. See [six-second-rule](six-second-rule.md).
