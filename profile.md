## Training history

- **Years in each discipline**, and peak weekly volumes ever reached.
- **Past races and events:** result, and *how they went*: pacing, where it fell apart, cramping, fuelling problems, what they'd change.
- **Breaks and layoffs:** when, how long, why (injury, illness, travel, life), and what was kept up during them.
- **What has worked and what hasn't:** plans, coaches, apps, structures they've tried.
