## Common gaps to check against the athlete's profile

Read [profile.md](../profile.md) (training history, injuries and niggles), then order whichever of these apply by priority:

1. **No lower-body work** — common in runners who only lift upper body; late-race cramping or fading can be a symptom. Fix: [MOC-running-strength](MOC-running-strength.md) Phase 1 rehab → Phase 2 light barbell → progressive.
2. **Push/pull bodybuilding split, no leg day** — replace with compound-focused split (see [MOC-upper-body](MOC-upper-body.md)).
3. **Avoids deadlifts (back injury history)** — trap-bar substitutes; see [MOC-injuries-management](MOC-injuries-management.md).
4. **Shoulder niggle** — see [shoulder-warmup](../concepts/shoulder-warmup.md) and escalation [posture-fix-routine](../concepts/posture-fix-routine.md).
