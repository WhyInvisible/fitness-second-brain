# Core training

> A strong core is load-bearing for: marathon stability (trunk control late in a race is the other half of the late-race-cramp story), L-sit / handstand / front lever progressions, lower-back resilience.
>
> Built around the **[McGill Big 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_McGill)** — evidence-based, **back-safe** (no spinal flexion). Especially important if the athlete has a lower-back history (see [profile.md](../profile.md) → Injuries and niggles).

## Daily core block (~10 min)

Do EVERY day. Per McGill: non-negotiable for someone with back-injury history. Mid-morning to dinnertime (NOT first thing — discs are swollen — and NOT right before bed). See [mcgill-big-3](../concepts/mcgill-big-3.md), [russian-descending-pyramid](../concepts/russian-descending-pyramid.md), [abdominal-brace](../concepts/abdominal-brace.md).

### Opener: cat-camel (greasing the joints)

- **7-8 slow cycles** (3-4 s each). NOT a stretch — never push end range. See cat-camel.md.

### The McGill Big 3 — Russian Descending Pyramid

All three exercises use **8-10 second holds** and the **6/4/2** descending-pyramid set scheme. **To progress: add ONE rep to each set. NEVER extend hold time.**

- **McGill curl-up**: Set 1 = 6 reps × 8 s, rest 30 s · Set 2 = 4 reps × 8 s, rest 20 s · Set 3 = 2 reps × 8 s. Done.
  - Lie on back, one knee bent, one leg straight. Hands **palms-down under lumbar** to maintain natural arch (do NOT flatten lumbar to floor — that's the Pilates mistake McGill rejects). Elbows hover. Brace abs (tune to mild). Lift head + shoulders ~1 inch. **NO sit-up motion** — imagine a scale reading zero under your head.
- **Side plank**: 6/4/2 × 8 s **per side**. Start with knee-down version (knees bent ~90°, hip-hinge forward to elevate). Progress to feet-stacked. Then rolling-plank (10 s side → 3 s front plank → 10 s other side = 1 rep).
- **Bird-dog**: 6/4/2 × 8 s **per side**. Find spine "sweet spot". Extend opposite arm + leg to horizontal — **push the heel away**, don't lift higher. Sweep the floor between holds.

Total time: ~10 min (cat-camel 30 s + curl-up ~3 min + side plank ~5 min + bird-dog ~5 min).

### Plus one rotating extra (~2 min) — Dicharry layer

Per [dynamic-anti-rotation-beats-static-planks](../concepts/dynamic-anti-rotation-beats-static-planks.md), once McGill Big 3 capacity is established, layer Dicharry's dynamic anti-rotation work for running transfer. Cycle through the extras below, one per day, a different focus each time. Skip the extra on days whose main session already includes core work (e.g. hanging knee raises on leg day, L-sit / compression / hollow body on pull day) and on long-run day. Place them around the athlete's week in [current-week.md](../plans/current-week.md).

| Rotation | Extra |
|---|---|
| A | Dead bug 3 × 8 per side (lie on back, opposite arm + leg lower slowly, brace abs) |
| B | Hollow body hold 3 × 20s (lie on back, arms overhead, low back pressed to floor, lift shoulders + legs) |
| C | Thread-the-needle plank 2 × 12 per side (Dicharry's anti-rotation drill — band perpendicular, untwist into plank, extend arm. More running-specific than Pallof) |
| D | Hanging leg raise 3 × 6 (L-sit prep — slow controlled, no swing) **or** hang spine twist 2 × 15 per side (Dicharry's spinal decompression + lateral-chain wiring) |

## Progressions

When the prescribed reps feel easy (clean form, no shake, you could do more):

- **McGill curl-up**: 8s → 10s → 12s holds
- **Side plank**: 20s → 30s → 45s; then star side plank (top arm/leg up); then with hip dips
- **Bird dog**: add 2s hold at top; then on hands not all fours; then add light DB in extended hand
- **Dead bug**: slow it down; then add a light weight in extended hand
- **Hollow body hold**: 20s → 30s → 45s → 60s
- **Hanging leg raise**: 6 → 8 → 10 strict; then to toes-to-bar

## Notes

- **Form > volume.** A clean 20s side plank beats a sloppy 45s.
- **No spinal flexion.** No crunches, no sit-ups, no bicycle crunches, no Russian twists, no supermans, no knees-to-chest. See [back-safe-exercise-contraindications](../concepts/back-safe-exercise-contraindications.md) for the full banned list.
- **Static planks are entry-level; dynamic anti-rotation is the goal.** Per Dicharry: "our end goal is not to impress our friends with a 4-minute plank, but to improve mobility while running." McGill Big 3 gives the daily base; once comfortable, layer in Dicharry's thread-the-needle plank, super-swiss-side-plank, bear walk, donkey toes. Don't chase 4-min planks — they don't transfer to running.
- **Loaded carries are core work too.** Suitcase carry, waiter carry, farmer carry train anti-lateral-flexion and anti-rotation under load. Bolt onto any leg or pull day — particularly the suitcase carry, which directly trains the late-marathon trunk-wobble pattern.
- **If lower back complains during *any* of these → stop.** Reassess. Possibly drop volume back or check form with a video.
- If recovery is suffering (sleep down, sluggish runs, niggles), **the rotating extras are the first thing to cut**. Keep the McGill Big 3 — they cost nothing and are pure prehab.
