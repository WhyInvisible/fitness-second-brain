# Climbing grip & forearm training

> Goal: develop the grip strength, forearm size, and hand callus base for climbing. This page assumes no hangboard yet; check the athlete's equipment in [profile.md](../profile.md) → Equipment and access.

## What this builds vs doesn't

**Achievable with a gym + a callus ball:**
- Grip endurance & strength (pull-up variants, towel work, plate pinches, farmer's carries)
- Forearm size (wrist curls, hanging work)
- Pinch grip (plate pinches)
- Some hand calluses (slow — months of chalked volume)

**Not achievable without a hangboard:**
- True crimp / open-hand finger strength
- Edge-specific finger conditioning
- Climbing-specific finger flexor adaptation

Once a hangboard is mounted, add a finger-strength protocol (see below).

## Block at end of pull day (~10 min)

Do at the end of the week's pull day, after the main pull work + L-sit but before cooldown.

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Towel pull-ups | 3 × 4–6 | Wrap a gym towel over the pull-up bar, grip the towel ends (one in each hand or both hands on one). Brutal for grip. Start with negatives if can't do 4 strict. |
| Plate pinch hold | 3 × 20s per hand | Pinch a 5–10 kg plate **smooth-side out**, thumb on one side, fingers on the other. Hold at hip. Start light. |
| Dead hang with chalk | 3 × max hold | Chalked grip, dead hang from bar. Target: build to 60s+. |
| Farmer's carry | 3 × 30 m | Heaviest DBs you can hold for the distance. Walk tall, don't let shoulders shrug. |

## Callus ball (passive — daily)

Squeeze 2–3 min total per day. Use during reading, calls, commute — pure free volume.

- Builds grip endurance + finger pad skin toughness
- Don't grip-train to failure — finger tendons are slow to adapt
- Mix up: full squeeze, individual finger work, rolling between palm and fingers

## Hangboard (when available)

A common home setup is a doorway pull-up bar or a plank mount. Once one is in place, add:
- 2× per week max
- 20–30 min sessions
- Repeaters protocol or max hangs (design it when the board is up)
- **Never on consecutive days** (finger tendons need 48+ hrs)
- Always after a thorough warm-up

## Stacking note

For a multi-discipline athlete, grip work stacks on top of everything else: running volume (running grips nothing), leg strength, calisthenics skills (pull-ups, muscle-up ladder), core work. The **medial elbow** is the bottleneck — golfer's elbow is the #1 injury when adding grip work on top of pull volume. So:

- Add the climbing block **gradually** — week 1: just chalked dead hangs + farmer's carries; week 2: add towel pull-ups; week 3: add plate pinches
- Do the [elbow cooldown](MOC-warmups-cooldowns.md#post-pull-day-cooldown--elbow--wrist-3-min) every pull day. Non-negotiable.
- If inner elbow starts complaining: drop towel pull-ups first (highest grip stress).
