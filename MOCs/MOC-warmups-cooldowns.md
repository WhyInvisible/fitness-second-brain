# Warm-ups & cooldowns (prehab)

> These are the *prehab* layer. Loaded into existing sessions — not extra sessions. Knees, ankles, shoulders, elbows all covered here.
>
> **Skipping the warm-up is how the shoulder twinges, the calf cramps, the knee niggles start.** Treat these as mandatory parts of the session, not optional.

## Pre-run warm-up (~90s)

Do before every run, including easy ones. Especially important AM when you're stiff.

- Ankle circles: 10 each direction, each foot
- Calf bounces (light, on the spot): 20
- Walking knee hugs: 8 per side
- Walking quad pulls: 8 per side
- Leg swings (forward/back + side): 10 each per side
- 30 seconds of marching on the spot, then go

Covers: ankle / calf / hip mobility. If one hamstring is habitually tighter (see [profile.md](../profile.md) → Injuries and niggles), the leg swings give it an equal turn with the other side.

## Pre-run form drills (Dicharry, ~60s extra)

Add to the warm-up at least 1× per week (especially before quality runs). They wire form cues so your body brings them to the run.

- **Push-versus-pull wall drill:** 30 s of stepping off a wall, driving with the back leg.
- **Elbow jab drill:** 30 s of arm swings while facing a tree / wall — forces backward arm swing.

## During-run reset (Dicharry)

Built into long runs once they reach ~25 km+:

- **Posture check** every mile, ~5 s: stand on one leg, balance heel-forefoot, drop ribs, drop arms, palms forward.
- **Shopping cart cue** between miles 15–25 of long runs (when form usually starts to break).

## Pre-leg-day warm-up (~5–6 min)

Do before every leg session. The point: wake up glutes, prep knees + back + foot tripod, before anything heavy.

- **Ankle belt mobility:** 20 reps per side **if dorsiflexion feels blocked in front of ankle** (Dicharry — joint glide, not stretch)
- Glute bridge or frog bridge: 2 × 10 (light, slow, squeeze at top)
- Bird dog: 2 × 6 per side
- **Donkey toes:** 60 s alternating (Dicharry — glute + anti-rotation pattern)
- **Foam roller drill:** 60 s (Dicharry — wires "screw foot down + screw hip out")
- Bodyweight squat or chair-of-death squat: 2 × 10
- Single-leg balance: 30s per leg (close eyes for harder)

Covers: ankle joint glide, glute activation, foot tripod, knee prep, hip + ankle, balance, back warm-up.

## Pre-upper-body warm-up (~5 min) — THE SHOULDER ONE

**Mandatory** before any upper-body session (push, pull, calisthenics skill work). This is the first-line fix for a shoulder twinge.

- Band pull-aparts: 2 × 15 (squeeze shoulder blades together, slow)
- Wall slides: 2 × 10 (back flat against wall, arms in "W" then slide up, keep wrists/elbows on wall)
- Scapular pull-ups (at the bar — shrug down, no arm bend): 2 × 8
- Banded external rotation: 2 × 12 per side (light band, elbow tucked at side, rotate hand outward slowly)
- Arm circles: 10 forward + 10 backward, each arm
- Cross-body stretch: 30s each arm
- Sleeper stretch: 30s each side

**If the twinge persists after 2 weeks → switch to Dicharry's full Posture Fix routine (~10 min) for 2 weeks:**
1. Basketball mobility 2–3 min
2. Travolta peanuts 2–3 min
3. Pec minor stretch 2–3 min per side
4. Overhead carry 3 × 45 s
5. Banded arm circles + band pull-aparts 20 reps each

**If still persisting after the Posture Fix block → see a physio.** Don't tough it out. Shoulder issues compound.

## Post-pull-day cooldown — elbow + wrist (~3 min)

Do after every pull day. Pull-up + grip + climbing-style work loads the medial elbow heavily — this prevents golfer's elbow.

- Reverse wrist curls (DB, very light 2.5–5 kg): 2 × 12 (palm down, slow eccentric)
- Wrist rotations: 10 each direction, each wrist
- Forearm flexor stretch: 30s per arm (arm extended, palm up, pull fingers down)
- Forearm extensor stretch: 30s per arm (arm extended, palm down, pull hand down)

## Notes

- Warm-ups are **non-negotiable**. They're how 80% of niggles never happen.
- If a session feels short on time, cut the *main* session — never skip the warm-up.
- If something feels actively wrong during a warm-up (sharp pain, not just stiffness), stop the session. Mobility work + yoga only that day.
- Reassess after 4–6 weeks: which warm-ups are still earning their place? Some you can drop once the relevant tissue is robust; some stay forever.
