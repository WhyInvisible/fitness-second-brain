# MOC: Training principles

The rules every plan, adjustment and weekly review is checked against. Notes marked `core: true` in their frontmatter are fed to the weekly-review model every Sunday.

## Core (always applied)

- [progressive-overload](../concepts/progressive-overload.md): ~10%/week ceiling; the slowest tissue sets the pace
- [recovery-is-part-of-training](../concepts/recovery-is-part-of-training.md): adaptation happens in recovery; trust trends, not single readings
- [dont-stack-high-intensity](../concepts/dont-stack-high-intensity.md): hard days hard, easy days easy, about 48 h between hard efforts
- [a-goal-wins-conflicts](../concepts/a-goal-wins-conflicts.md): one A-goal, and it wins every conflict

## Add as the workspace grows

Each new principle distilled from a source gets its own note in `concepts/` and a line here. Build a new MOC (`MOC-running.md`, `MOC-strength.md`, `MOC-injuries.md` ...) once a theme has five or more notes.
