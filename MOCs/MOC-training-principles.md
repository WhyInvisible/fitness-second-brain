# MOC — Training principles

> Atomic principles. Each one is a single concept in [concepts/](../concepts/). This MOC groups them.

The "why" behind any plan in this workspace. When in doubt — when adjusting a week, debating a workout, or sense-checking advice — come back here, then click through to the specific concept note.

## Core (always applied)

These four apply to every week, whatever the discipline mix. Notes marked `core: true` in their frontmatter are fed to every Sunday weekly review.

- [The A-goal wins conflicts](../concepts/a-goal-wins-conflicts.md) — one A-goal in goals.md; when disciplines compete for recovery or a day, it wins, and the plan says so
- [Progressive overload — but slowly](../concepts/progressive-overload.md) — ~10% a week is the soft ceiling on volume or load increases; tendons and joints adapt slowest
- [Recovery is part of training](../concepts/recovery-is-part-of-training.md) — adaptation happens in rest; under-recovering wastes the work, and it's the most likely failure mode in a multi-discipline week
- [Don't stack high-intensity work on the same day](../concepts/dont-stack-high-intensity.md) — hard run + heavy lift + hard skill work in one 24 h draw on the same recovery pool; default to one hard stimulus a day
