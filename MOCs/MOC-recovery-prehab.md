## Daily movement breadth (Le Corre, ~5–10 min)

For the lowest-cost / highest-leverage mobility + corrective work:

- Quadrupedal crawl variants — daily 5-min mixed crawl block; no spine compression, addresses cross-body coordination + hip mobility + scapular control
- Balance beam walk — daily 5-min on a 2x4 board in the flat; single-leg stability, ankle proprioception, mind-body engagement
- Natural get-up variants — daily 2-min get-up practice; longevity-predictor skill (Brazilian SRT)
- [Crawling as corrective exercise](../concepts/crawling-as-corrective-exercise.md) — the *why*

## Athlete-specific prehab focus areas

The athlete's own focus areas live in [profile.md](../profile.md) → Injuries and niggles. Common patterns and the default response:

- **Lower-back injury history** — back-safe everything; see [MOC-injuries-management](MOC-injuries-management.md)
- **Hamstring asymmetry (one side tighter or weaker)** — asymmetric mobility focus, single-leg strength priority
- **Shoulder twinge** — mandatory 5-min shoulder warmup before upper-body work; escalate to Posture Fix if it persists

See [MOC-injuries-management](MOC-injuries-management.md) for the structured response to each.
