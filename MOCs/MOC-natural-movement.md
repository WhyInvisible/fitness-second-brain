# MOC — Natural Movement (Le Corre / MovNat framework)

> Hub for the Le Corre / MovNat framework — practical physical capability across the full evolutionary movement vocabulary. **Most relevant for travel / no-gym phases and for an off-season movement-variety block after the A-race.** Selective elements (daily crawl, beam walk, precision jump warm-ups, carry variants) apply during a marathon build at near-zero recovery cost.

## When this MOC matters

| Phase | Le Corre relevance | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Marathon build — base phase** | Low–medium | Daily ground-movement block (5 min crawl + 5 min beam walk) + 1 weekly carry variant rotation |
| **Travel / no-gym phase** | **PRIMARY** | Full natural-movement program — no gym, varied terrain, daily practice |
| **Marathon-specific build** | Low | Maintain daily ground block; pause new additions |
| **Power block (~6 weeks pre-taper)** | Medium | Add precision-jump warm-ups (~3 min) to strength sessions |
| **Taper** | Low | Maintain only |
| **Off-season (after the A-race)** | **SECONDARY PRIMARY** | 1–2 weekly natural-movement sessions alongside a calisthenics or strength block |

## Session templates

### Daily morning ritual (5–10 min, year-round)

1. 30 sec each, mixed crawl variants (knee-hand → bear → inverted bear) — ~2 min
2. 1 min beam walk forward + 1 min backward + 30 sec single-leg balance each side — ~3 min
3. 1 natural get-up variant each side — ~1 min

Costs nothing. Useful for an athlete with a hamstring, shoulder or lower-back history (see [profile.md](../profile.md) → Injuries and niggles), since it touches all three at once.

### Travel / no-gym template (~30 min daily)

For weeks with no gym access but varied terrain and real environments (travel, holidays, outdoor blocks). This is Le Corre's natural habitat. Use the full method.

**Warm-up (5 min):**
- Breath control: 1 min nasal abdominal breathing
- Knee-hand crawl: 30 sec forward + 30 sec backward
- Beam walk on a curb / line / log: 1 min
- Hip airplane each side: 30 sec

**Emphasis (15–20 min) — rotate daily:**
- **Day A: ground + balance** — multiple crawl variants on grass / dirt / sand; beam walk progressions on a fallen log; get-up variants on uneven ground
- **Day B: lift + carry** — find local heavy objects (stones, branches); practice deadlift pattern with odd objects; rotate carry holds across 200 m walks
- **Day C: airborne** — precision jumps between natural targets; downward jumps from low elevations onto stable surfaces; stick the landing
- **Day D: climb + hang** — find a tree, rope, or playground bars; hang variants; climbing if available; basic ascents

**Cool-down (5 min):**
- 1 min slow knee-hand crawl
- 2 min seated breathwork
- 2 min ground positions (sit variations — open bent / cross / side bent)

**Plus maintenance running** if the phase falls inside a build (maintenance volume, no quality sessions — see [season plan](../plans/season-plan.md)).

### Off-season weekly slot (~45 min)

Once weekly after the A-race, alongside a calisthenics or strength block. Designed as a *combo session* (Le Corre's preferred format):

10-min flow circuit, repeat × 3:
- 30 yd bear crawl
- 1 precision jump (mark to mark)
- 10 sec single-leg balance hold each side
- 1 natural get-up variant
- 30 sec one-arm carry (KB or stone) each side
- 15 sec dead hang or active hang

Builds transitions between skills, develops adaptability, hits all skill categories in one session.

### Marathon-build precision-jump warm-up (power block, ~3 min)

Drop into strength session warm-up, weekly:
- 5 leg-swing precision jumps (each leg leading)
- 5 two-foot power jumps to a small target
- Stick every landing. Quality > quantity.
